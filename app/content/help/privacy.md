Bardic Torch keeps as little about you as it can. You can read the whole site without an account, and we never show you adverts.

## What we store

If you log in, we store:

- your **Auth0 user ID**, a code such as `google-oauth2|123…` that tells us it is you. We don’t ask Google or Facebook for your name or email address, and we don’t receive them;
- your **display name**, which starts as ‘Bard’ and a short code and which you can change in [Settings](/settings);
- your **edits**. Every edit is public, with the display name you have now, in the page’s history, and is shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](/help/licence).

If you write to us through the [contact form](/contact), we store your message with the email address you give, and your name if you give it. Only the moderators read them, and we use your email address only to reply.

## Who else sees what

- **Auth0** signs you in, with Google or Facebook. Auth0 and the service you choose see that you logged in.
- **Cloudflare** hosts the site, so it handles every page you visit, as any host does. Its **Turnstile** check on the contact form makes sure a person, not a bot, sent the message.
- **Sentry**, if it is turned on, receives reports of errors in the site. They leave out who you are, your IP address, cookies and anything you typed.

## Cookies

There are no analytics or advertising cookies. We use only:

- the **sign-in cookies**: your session while you are logged in, and two short-lived ones that last only while you log in;
- the **theme cookie**, which remembers if you chose light or dark.

## Removing your data or opting out

To ask us to remove your account or anything else about you, [write to us through the contact form](/contact?kind=opt_out). You don’t need an account. Your past edits stay part of the pages they changed, but you can change the name they show at any time.

If there is a page about you and you would rather not have one, you can ask for that too. See [What if I don’t want a page?](/help/faq-for-bards#what-if-i-dont-want-a-page) in the FAQ for bards.
