Bardic Torch keeps as little about you as it can. You can read the whole site without an account, and we never show you adverts.

## What we store

If you log in, we store:

- your **Auth0 user ID**, a code such as `google-oauth2|123…` that tells us it is you;
- your **name** on your Google or Facebook account, which we receive through Auth0 each time you log in. We show it as your **display name**, publicly with your edits. We don’t receive your email address;
- your **edits**. Every edit is public, with your display name as it is now, in the page’s history, and is shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](/help/licence).

If you write to us through the [contact form](/contact), we store your message with the email address you give, and your name if you give it. Only the moderators read them, and we use your email address only to reply.

## Who else sees what

- **Auth0** signs you in, with Google or Facebook, and passes your name on to us. Auth0 and the service you choose see that you logged in.
- **Cloudflare** hosts the site, so it handles every page you visit, as any host does. Its **Turnstile** check on the contact form makes sure a person, not a bot, sent the message.
- **Sentry**, if it is turned on, receives reports of errors in the site. They leave out who you are, your IP address, cookies and anything you typed.

## Cookies

There are no analytics or advertising cookies. We use only:

- the **sign-in cookies**: your session while you are logged in, and two short-lived ones that last only while you log in;
- the **theme cookie**, which remembers if you chose light or dark.

## Removing your data, or not having a page

To ask us to remove your account or anything else about you, [write to us through the contact form](/contact?kind=opt_out). You don’t need an account. Your past edits stay part of the pages they changed. They show your name as it is on Google or Facebook, so changing your name there changes the name your past edits show, from the next time you log in.

If there is a page about you and you would rather not have one, publish a public statement that you prefer not to have a page, and [send us the link](/contact?kind=opt_out). See [What if I don’t want to have a page at Bardic Torch?](/help/faq-for-bards#what-if-i-dont-want-to-have-a-page-at-bardic-torch) in the FAQ for bards.
