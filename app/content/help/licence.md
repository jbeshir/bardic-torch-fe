Bardic Torch is a community project, and its text belongs to the community too. This page explains what you may do with it, where it came from, and what it doesn’t cover.

:::infobox{title="At a glance"}

- **Your contributions:** released under CC BY-SA 4.0 when you save them
- **Imported text:** from the [Bardic Torch wiki](https://bardictorch.miraheze.org), also CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Linked works:** belong to their creators. Our licence doesn’t cover them

:::

## Our licence

The text on Bardic Torch is available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) (CC BY-SA 4.0). That covers notes, descriptions, licensing and etiquette summaries, help pages and other written content.

Under this licence you may copy, share and adapt that text for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you:

- **give credit**: name Bardic Torch and its contributors, link to the page you used, and say if you changed anything
- **share alike**: release your adapted version under CC BY-SA 4.0 too
- **add no extra restrictions** that would stop others doing the same

A credit line could look like this: ‘Adapted from “Page title” on Bardic Torch, by its contributors, under CC BY-SA 4.0’, with links to the page and the licence.

## What the licence doesn’t cover

Bardic Torch is an index. We never host lyrics, the text of works, recordings, video or other files. The songs, stories, poems and dances we link to belong to their creators, under whatever terms they chose.

- A piece’s licensing notes tell you what its **creator** has said, with a link to where they said it. Always check that source before you perform, record, print, adapt or sell a work.
- Our CC BY-SA licence gives you **no** rights in the works themselves.
- If a creator hasn’t said anything, their work is protected by the default copyright law in their country.

## Where our text came from

Bardic Torch began as a wiki at [bardictorch.miraheze.org](https://bardictorch.miraheze.org), built by the bardic community. Its text was published under CC BY-SA 4.0, and we imported it under that licence when this site launched.

- **Pieces and bards:** each imported page credits the original wiki’s contributors in its history and footer.
- **Help pages:** these are adapted from the wiki’s own help and policy pages. They have been rewritten for this site, and some rules have changed, for example no lyrics are hosted at all. Each help page names its source page and links to it.
- **The original wiki stays online** where it is, with its full page histories.

## Thank you

Bardic Torch wouldn’t exist without the people who built the original wiki. Thank you to every editor who added a bard, a piece, a link or a category. Special thanks to Firinn, the wiki’s lead maintainer, who made most of its edits.

You can see everyone who contributed in the [original wiki’s list of users](https://bardictorch.miraheze.org/wiki/Special:ListUsers) and in the history of each page there.

## Your contributions

When you edit Bardic Torch, you agree to release your contribution under CC BY-SA 4.0. The edit form will remind you. Your edits are credited to you in each page’s public history.

Never paste in lyrics, the text of a work, or someone else’s writing. Link to it instead. See the [contributing guide](/help/contributing).

## Not an official site

Bardic Torch is community-maintained and not affiliated with the Society for Creative Anachronism or any other organisation. That includes re-enactment, LARP, fan, SF&F and filk groups, conventions and musical groups.

## Questions and takedowns

If you think something on Bardic Torch infringes your rights, or you’d like a link removed, [contact us through our form](https://forms.gle/wUwWnMKtWZSUisWCA). We can remove or hide a link, but the content will stay online wherever it is hosted. See the [FAQ for bards](/help/faq-for-bards) for more.
